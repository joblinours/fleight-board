# Commercial Pricing Terms

This document sets out the standard pricing of the [Couche 8 Commercial License](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md). It does not replace the [Couche 8 Community License (C8CL)](LICENSE.md).

Commercial use of the Software requires a valid Commercial License issued by Couche 8 ASBL.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the standard annual pricing is based on the total number of Employees of the Commercial Organization:

| Organization size       |                          Annual license fee |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------: |
| 1–50 Employees          |                             €7 per Employee |
| 51–150 Employees        |                             €9 per Employee |
| 151–250 Employees       |                            €12 per Employee |
| More than 250 Employees | €4,000 per year, unlimited Authorized Users |

## 1. Employee Count

For organizations with 250 or fewer Employees, the applicable fee is calculated by multiplying the number of Employees by the applicable per-Employee rate.

The applicable tier is determined by the total number of Employees of the Commercial Organization, not by the number of users actively connected to the Software.

For organizations with more than 250 Employees, the €4,000 annual license provides unlimited Authorized Users, subject to the terms of the applicable Commercial License.

## 2. Unlimited User Tier

The >250 Employee tier provides unlimited Authorized Users within the licensed Commercial Organization.

No additional per-user fee applies once the organization has purchased the €4,000 annual license.

The unlimited-user provision applies only to the licensed organization and does not authorize use by unrelated organizations, subsidiaries, customers, or third parties unless expressly permitted by the Commercial License.

## 3. Annual License

Commercial licenses are granted on an annual basis unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The license fee covers the applicable licensing period specified in the Commercial License.

Renewal fees may be updated by Couche 8 and will be communicated before renewal.

## 4. Affiliates and Subsidiaries

The Employee count normally applies to the legal entity purchasing the Commercial License.

Subsidiaries, parent companies, affiliates, franchises, or other legally separate entities are not automatically covered unless explicitly included in the Commercial License.

Couche 8 may offer group or enterprise agreements covering multiple legal entities.

## 5. Verification

Couche 8 may reasonably request information necessary to determine the applicable licensing tier.

A Commercial Organization must provide accurate information regarding its Employee count when purchasing or renewing a Commercial License.

The purpose of this requirement is solely to determine the applicable licensing fee.

## 6. Educational and Personal Use

The commercial pricing described in this section does not apply to:

* Personal Use;
* Educational Institutions using the Software for educational purposes;
* eligible Non-Profit Organizations using the Software for permitted non-commercial purposes.

Such use remains free under the terms of the Couche 8 Community License.

## 7. Commercial Agreement

The pricing described above represents Couche 8's standard commercial pricing.

Couche 8 may enter into separate written agreements providing:

* discounted pricing;
* enterprise agreements;
* multi-year agreements;
* support contracts;
* service-level agreements;
* hosting services;
* professional services;
* or other commercial terms.

Where a written Commercial License contains different pricing or licensing terms, those terms take precedence for that customer.
