This document defines the standard commercial licensing terms for software distributed by **Couche 8 ASBL** ("Couche 8") under the **Couche 8 Community License (C8CL)**.

The C8CL allows free Personal Use and Educational Use. Commercial Organizations must obtain a valid Commercial License before using the Software for commercial or professional purposes.

---

## 1. Commercial License Requirement

A Commercial Organization must hold a valid Commercial License to use the Software for:

* internal business operations;
* professional activities;
* commercial projects;
* customer or client work;
* paid services;
* corporate collaboration;
* commercial training;
* commercial research;
* or any other Commercial Use as defined by the C8CL.

A Commercial License is granted to a specific legal entity unless otherwise stated in writing.

---

# 2. Standard Pricing

The standard annual Commercial License fee is determined by the total number of Employees within the licensed organization.

| Organization size    |                             Annual license fee |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------: |
| **1–50 Employees**    |                     **€7 per Employee / year** |
| **51–150 Employees**  |                     **€9 per Employee / year** |
| **151–250 Employees** |                    **€12 per Employee / year** |
| **251+ Employees**    | **€4,000 / year — unlimited Authorized Users** |

All prices are expressed in euros and are exclusive of any applicable taxes, VAT, or similar charges.

The standard pricing is also summarized in [PRICING.md](PRICING.md).

Organizations with more than 250 Employees may obtain the **Unlimited Organization License** for:

* unlimited Authorized Users;

* unlimited use of the Software within the licensed organization;
* self-hosting rights, subject to the applicable license terms;
* access to the standard Software functionality included in the licensed version.

No additional per-user or per-employee fee applies under the €4,000 Unlimited Organization License.

For example:

| Employees | Annual fee |

# 6. Employee Count

For organizations with 250 or fewer Employees, the applicable annual fee is calculated using the organization's total number of Employees.

The licensing fee is **not calculated based solely on the number of active users of the Software**.

A Commercial Organization must therefore report its applicable Employee count when purchasing or renewing a Commercial License.

Couche 8 may reasonably request information necessary to verify the applicable licensing tier.

The organization is responsible for providing accurate information.

---

# 7. What Counts as an Employee

For the purposes of determining the applicable licensing tier, an Employee includes individuals who:

* are directly employed by the organization;
* regularly work for the organization;
* are contractors or temporary workers using the Software as part of their work;
* or otherwise qualify as members of the organization's workforce under the organization's applicable employment structure.

Couche 8 may agree to alternative definitions of Employee in a written Commercial License.

---

# 8. Authorized Users

An Authorized User is any individual authorized by the Commercial Organization to access or use the Software.

Authorized Users may include:

* Employees;
* contractors;
* temporary workers;
* interns;
* administrators;
* or other individuals acting on behalf of the licensed organization.

For organizations with 250 or fewer Employees, the number of Authorized Users and other usage conditions may be specified in the applicable Commercial License.

For organizations using the **€4,000 Unlimited Organization License**, there is no additional fee based on the number of Authorized Users.

---

# 9. Legal Entity

A Commercial License covers the legal entity identified in the license agreement.

The license does not automatically extend to:

* parent companies;
* subsidiaries;
* sister companies;
* affiliates;
* franchises;
* customers;
* suppliers;
* or other legally separate organizations.

Such entities may require their own Commercial License unless they are explicitly included in a written agreement with Couche 8.

Couche 8 may offer group or enterprise agreements covering multiple legal entities.

# 10. Self-Hosting

A Commercial Organization may self-host the Software provided that it holds a valid Commercial License.

The license permits the organization to deploy the Software on infrastructure that it owns or controls, subject to the applicable Commercial License.

Self-hosting does not remove the requirement to obtain a Commercial License.

Couche 8 may provide separate licensing terms for:

* hosting companies;

# 14. Development and Testing

A Commercial Organization holding a valid Commercial License may use the Software for development, testing, staging, and production purposes.

Development and testing environments operated by the licensed organization are covered by the same Commercial License unless otherwise specified.

# 15. Commercial License Duration

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Commercial Licenses are valid for **one year** from the effective date.

The license must be renewed before the end of the licensing period to continue Commercial Use.

Couche 8 may offer:

* monthly licenses;
* multi-year licenses;
* enterprise agreements;
* special contractual terms;

at its discretion.

---

# 16. Renewal

Unless otherwise agreed, the Commercial License must be renewed annually.

The applicable licensing fee at renewal may be based on the organization's Employee count at the time of renewal.

Couche 8 may modify its standard commercial pricing for future licensing periods.

Any pricing change does not retroactively modify an already-paid licensing period.

---

# 17. Growth During a License Period

If the number of Employees of a Commercial Organization changes during a licensing period, the organization remains covered by the licensing tier specified in its current Commercial License unless otherwise agreed.

At renewal, the applicable pricing tier may be recalculated based on the organization's current Employee count.

Organizations that exceed 250 Employees may transition to the €4,000 Unlimited Organization License at renewal or earlier if agreed with Couche 8.

---

# 18. Reduction in Organization Size

A reduction in Employee count during an active licensing period does not automatically result in a refund or retroactive price adjustment.

At renewal, the applicable licensing tier may be recalculated based on the organization's Employee count.

Specific contractual arrangements may provide different terms.

# 24. Prohibited Circumvention

A Commercial Organization must not intentionally circumvent the licensing model.

Examples include:

* splitting one organization into multiple artificial entities solely to obtain a lower licensing tier;
* deliberately misreporting Employee counts;
* creating multiple accounts to avoid applicable licensing restrictions;
* sharing accounts between unrelated individuals;
* using subsidiaries or affiliates that are not covered by the license;
* or otherwise attempting to avoid applicable licensing fees.

This section does not prevent legitimate corporate restructuring or organizational arrangements.
